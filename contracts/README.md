# Agency Contracts & Legal Documents Repository

This directory contains standardized contract templates, service level agreements (SLAs), operational terms, and business policies for Boosting Agency BD.

## Governance & Safety Notice
- **Confidentiality:** Individual client records, proprietary ad account IDs, signed master agreements, and private invoices must NEVER be committed to public repositories.
- **Wording Integrity:** All contract language reflects the official terms codified in `lib/terms-data.ts` and `data/knowledge-base/policies.json`.
- **Jurisdiction:** All contracts and retainers are governed by and construed under the applicable laws and judicial courts of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.

## Folder Contents

### 1. `contracts/templates/`
- `digital-marketing-retainer-agreement.md`: Standard monthly campaign management & ad operations contract.
- `ad-spend-dollar-topup-agreement.md`: Balance loading, exchange rate pegging, and ad account policy agreement.
- `server-side-tracking-capi-sla.md`: Technical scope of work and data accuracy SLA for Meta Pixel & CAPI implementations.
- `non-disclosure-and-confidentiality-template.md`: Mutual confidentiality and non-disclosure terms for client business assets.

### 2. `contracts/documents/`
- `agency-terms-and-conditions.md`: Complete 10-clause operational terms, billing guidelines, and compliance rules.
- `privacy-and-data-protection-policy.md`: Customer data handling, tracking consent, and privacy standards.
- `refund-and-cancellation-policy.md`: Official refund eligibility, dispute resolution, and campaign pausing policies.
- `scope-of-work-specifications.md`: Deliverable boundaries, turnaround times, and revision quotas.
