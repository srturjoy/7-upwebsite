# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## 1. Setup & Service Fees
Setup fees (Page setup, Pixel integration, custom strategy work) are non-refundable once technical labor has commenced.

## 2. Media Spend Top-ups
Unspent ad account balance may be paused or refunded minus any incurred payment gateway fees (typically 1.5% - 2.5%) upon 7-day written request.

## 3. Performance Disclaimers
In professional advertising, traffic volume and ad impressions are managed diligently; however, organic buying decisions depend on client product, pricing, and fulfillment.
